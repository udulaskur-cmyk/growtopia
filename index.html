// What is Growtopia?

Plant. Harvest.
Build Anything.

Growtopia is a 2D sandbox MMO game where your imagination is the only limit. Plant seeds, grow trees, smash blocks, collect items, and construct anything from a simple house to a sprawling parkour course — all in real time with friends from around the globe.



Every item in the game comes from seeds. Splice seeds together to discover thousands of combinations. Trade your rarest finds in the player-driven economy powered by World Locks and Gems.

// The Economy

The World
Runs on Locks.

World Locks are the backbone of Growtopia's economy. Every rare item, exclusive cosmetic, and powerful tool has a market value determined entirely by the players. Become a trader, merchant, or even a mogul.


The game is free to play, and the rarest items can be earned through skill, smart trading, and dedicated farming — no pay-to-win.
